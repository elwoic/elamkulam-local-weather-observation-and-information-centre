About these observations

What these measurements mean

Temperature
Air temperature measured at the ELWOIC local observation site.
Feels like temperature
An apparent-temperature estimate of how current conditions may feel to the human body. It accounts for temperature, humidity and wind.
Humidity
Relative humidity: how much moisture is in the air compared to the maximum the air can hold at the current temperature.
Wind
Speed, direction and gust readings describe air movement measured at the observation site itself.
Rainfall
Precipitation detected by the local weather station. Rainfall can vary considerably over short distances, even within Elamkulam.
Atmospheric pressure
Air pressure at the observation site. Changes in pressure are a useful early signal when weather conditions are developing.
Air quality
Measurements from ELWOIC's outdoor air-quality monitor, including particulate matter (PM2.5, PM10) where available.

Reading this data carefully

Local observation limitations

About local observations

The measurements shown here represent conditions at the ELWOIC observation site. Weather can vary between locations, even within Elamkulam — so the ELWOIC observation system isn't intended to represent every location in the area.

Readings can also be affected by sensor characteristics, installation conditions, temporary communication problems and equipment availability.

External weather information

Some information on ELWOIC may originate from external weather services or publicly available meteorological information, rather than from ELWOIC's own equipment. Where it's used, it's identified separately from ELWOIC's direct local observations, and it may follow a different geographical coverage, update schedule and methodology.

Read more on How ELWOIC Works.

About ELWOIC Weather Information

ELWOIC is an independent, student-run, non-profit local weather observation and information project operating in Elamkulam, Malappuram, Kerala.

The information on this page is intended for local weather awareness. It should not be treated as a replacement for official weather warnings or emergency instructions issued by competent authorities.

How to read this forecast

Understanding the numbers

Rain probability
The likelihood of measurable precipitation during the forecast period, according to the relevant forecast source. It isn't the percentage of Elamkulam that will get rain, or a guarantee that rain will occur.
Rainfall amount
The expected precipitation accumulation for the period shown. Actual rainfall can differ from the forecast.
Forecast temperature
Expected air temperature for the relevant location and time. Actual temperatures can differ, since local weather can develop differently than forecast.

Forecast vs observation

Forecast and observation are different

A forecast describes conditions expected in the future. An observation describes conditions actually measured at the observation site.

ELWOIC uses both: current observations give context for interpreting developing weather, while forecasts describe what's expected next. As new observations and updated forecast information arrive, the expected weather situation may change.

Forecast sources

Where does the forecast come from?

ELWOIC's forecast information may draw on numerical weather prediction output, external weather-service forecasts, radar and satellite information where available, local observations, and ELWOIC's own automated short-term analysis.

  • ELWOIC's own atmospheric analysis engine, combining live local observations with short-term trend signals
  • External weather-service forecast data (used specifically for forward-looking rain probability)
  • Relevant numerical model output, examined through available interfaces and visualisations where applicable

Forecast uncertainty

Why can forecasts change?

Weather forecasts are estimates of future atmospheric conditions. They can change as new observations come in, as forecast models update, or as weather systems develop differently than earlier expected.

Short-term forecasts can also be affected by rapidly developing thunderstorms, local rainfall and other small-scale weather processes that are difficult to predict precisely.

For this reason, a forecast should be understood as an assessment of expected conditions, not a guarantee of what will occur.

Records methodology

How ELWOIC weather records are produced

Weather records are generated from observations received by the ELWOIC observation system and stored in ELWOIC's data infrastructure. The data is processed to calculate summaries such as averages, totals, minimums, maximums and other statistics.

Historical records may be updated when delayed data becomes available or when data-processing corrections are made.

Records represent observations from the ELWOIC observation site and should not be interpreted as measurements for every location in Elamkulam.

Data quality

Missing or incomplete data

About gaps in the record

Historical records may contain periods with missing or incomplete observations due to equipment, network, power or data-service interruptions.

Where incomplete data affects a calculation, ELWOIC identifies the limitation rather than treating the available measurements as a complete record.
