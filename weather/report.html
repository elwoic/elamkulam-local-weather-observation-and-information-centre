About these observations

What these measurements mean

Temperature
Air temperature measured at the ELWOIC local observation site.
Feels like temperature
An apparent-temperature estimate accounting for temperature, humidity and wind.
Humidity
Relative humidity: how much moisture is in the air compared to the maximum the air can hold at the current temperature.
Wind
Speed, direction and gust readings measured at the observation site itself.
Rainfall
Precipitation detected by the local weather station. Rainfall can vary considerably over short distances, even within Elamkulam.
Atmospheric pressure
Air pressure at the observation site. Changes in pressure are a useful early signal when weather conditions are developing.
Air quality
Measurements from ELWOIC's outdoor air-quality monitor, including particulate matter (PM2.5, PM10) where available.

Reading this data carefully

Local observation limitations

About local observations

The measurements shown here represent conditions at the ELWOIC observation site. Weather can vary between locations, even within Elamkulam. Readings can also be affected by sensor characteristics, installation conditions, temporary communication problems and equipment availability.

External weather information

Some information on ELWOIC may originate from external weather services or publicly available meteorological information, rather than from ELWOIC's own equipment. Where it's used, it's identified separately from ELWOIC's direct local observations.

About ELWOIC

ELWOIC is an independent, student-run, non-profit local weather observation and information project operating in Elamkulam, Malappuram, Kerala. The information on this page is intended for local weather awareness and should not replace official warnings issued by competent authorities.

How to read this forecast

Forecast methodology

Climate condition
A qualitative label produced by ELWOIC's prediction engine, summarising what the atmosphere is likely to do over the next 3 hours based on converging signals — not a single-parameter reading.
Confidence percentage
The engine's estimated probability that the forecast condition label will prove correct. Lower confidence means signals are mixed or ambiguous.
Barometric tendency (3h)
Change in relative pressure over the past 3 hours. Falling pressure is a key signal for developing rain; rising pressure indicates improving conditions.
Vapour pressure deficit (VPD)
The gap between actual atmospheric moisture and saturation. Near-zero VPD means the air is almost fully loaded and can hold little additional moisture — a key pre-rain signal.
Reissued forecast
A forecast is reissued (rather than carried forward) when the condition label changes, confidence shifts by 10 percentage points or more, or a new critical alert is triggered.

ELWOIC's forecasts are automated and should be understood as an assessment of expected conditions, not a guarantee. For official warnings, refer to the India Meteorological Department.

Records methodology

How ELWOIC weather records are produced

Weather records are generated from observations received by the ELWOIC observation system. The data is processed to calculate averages, totals, minimums, maximums and other statistics. Records may be updated when delayed data becomes available or when data-processing corrections are made.

Records represent observations from the ELWOIC observation site and should not be interpreted as measurements for every location in Elamkulam.
