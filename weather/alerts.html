About the alert types

What are EWA and EWAE?

🌦️ EWA — ELWOIC Weather Alert

Used for regular weather information and alerts concerning expected, developing or changing weather conditions affecting Elamkulam.

  • Expected rainfall
  • Changes in rainfall conditions
  • Increasing or decreasing heat
  • Developing weather patterns
  • Short-term local weather situations
🚨 EWAE — Emergency / Event Alert

Used for shorter-notice or more significant weather situations requiring more immediate public attention.

  • A significant weather event approaching
  • Rapidly developing rainfall
  • An ongoing event requiring immediate attention
  • Other locally relevant situations assessed as requiring an emergency alert
Important distinction The use of an EWAE designation describes ELWOIC's publication category. It does not make the alert an official government emergency warning.

Methodology

How ELWOIC alerts are prepared

ELWOIC alerts are prepared using available information at the time of assessment. This may include local observations from the ELWOIC observation system, processed weather data, available numerical weather prediction information, radar and satellite imagery, external weather-service information and direct observation of developing local conditions.

Alert assessments may be updated when new observations or weather information become available.

Before you read

Reading the historical archive

Archived alerts represent the information and assessment available to ELWOIC at the time each alert was published. Weather conditions can develop differently from an earlier assessment, and later observations may differ from an earlier expectation.

An archived alert should be understood as a historical publication, not a current forecast or active warning.

Archive limitations

About this archive

The archive contains ELWOIC publications that have been preserved in the ELWOIC information system. Older publications may have differences in formatting or available metadata depending on how they were originally published.

Historical publications represent ELWOIC's assessment at the time of publication and should not be used as current weather information.

Important: ELWOIC is an independent, student-run, non-profit weather observation and information initiative. ELWOIC alerts are independent informational assessments and are not official government weather warnings. For current official warnings, emergency instructions and authoritative weather advisories, refer to the relevant competent authorities, including the India Meteorological Department.
