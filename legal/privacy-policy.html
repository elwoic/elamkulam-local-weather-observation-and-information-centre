Privacy Policy

Elamkulam Local Weather Observation & Information Centre (ELWOIC)

Effective Date: 26 July 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to the official website of Elamkulam Local Weather Observation & Information Centre (ELWOIC).

ELWOIC is an independent, student-operated community weather information initiative based in Elamkulam, Malappuram District, Kerala, India. The initiative aims to promote local weather awareness through weather observations, forecasting, educational resources, community participation, and related information services.

While ELWOIC has been acknowledged by Elamkulam Grama Panchayat, it operates independently and is not owned, operated, managed, or administered by the Government of India, the Government of Kerala, the India Meteorological Department (IMD), Elamkulam Grama Panchayat, or any other Government authority unless expressly stated.

This Privacy Policy explains how ELWOIC collects, uses, stores, protects, and manages information submitted through this website and related ELWOIC services.

By accessing this website or using any ELWOIC service, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Visitors are welcome to browse this website without registering or providing personal information. ELWOIC collects personal information only when it is voluntarily submitted by a user.

Information may be collected through:

  • Contact Forms
  • ELWOIC Registration Forms
  • Weather Report Submission Forms
  • Feedback Forms
  • Data Request Forms
  • Volunteer Forms
  • Service Request Forms
  • Email communication
  • Telephone communication
  • Other forms or services provided by ELWOIC

Depending on the service being used, information collected may include:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Mobile or Telephone Number
  • Address or Location (where required)
  • Google Account Name (where Google Sign-In is required)
  • Google Email Address
  • Weather observations submitted by users
  • Photographs or supporting files voluntarily uploaded
  • Any additional information voluntarily provided by the user

ELWOIC does not require visitors to provide personal information simply to browse publicly accessible pages of this website.

3. Google Sign-In

To reduce spam, duplicate submissions, false weather reports, misuse of services, and automated abuse, certain ELWOIC forms or services may require users to sign in using a Google Account.

When Google Sign-In is used, ELWOIC typically receives only the information that you authorize Google to share, including:

  • Your Google display name
  • Your Google email address

ELWOIC does not receive your Google password and does not gain access to your Google Account beyond the information you choose to share.

4. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted to ELWOIC is used solely for legitimate operational purposes related to ELWOIC. Depending on the service, information may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries
  • Process registrations
  • Provide requested services
  • Verify submitted weather reports
  • Contact users regarding weather-related matters
  • Provide updates relating to ELWOIC services
  • Improve website functionality and user experience
  • Prevent misuse or abuse of ELWOIC services
  • Maintain operational records
  • Respond to legal or security requirements where applicable

ELWOIC does not sell, rent, lease, or trade personal information. Personal information is not shared with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Information may only be disclosed where:

  • required by applicable law;
  • necessary to protect ELWOIC systems or services;
  • necessary to investigate misuse of ELWOIC services;
  • or where the user has provided explicit consent.

5. Registration with ELWOIC

Registration with ELWOIC is entirely voluntary. Registration is not required to browse publicly accessible sections of this website.

Users who voluntarily register with ELWOIC may receive services including:

  • Weather notifications
  • Community weather participation
  • ELWOIC announcements
  • Weather observation programmes
  • Requested information services
  • Other ELWOIC-related communications

By registering, users consent to being contacted by ELWOIC regarding matters directly related to their registration, requested services, weather information, or ELWOIC activities. Registration information is used solely for ELWOIC operations and is not used for unrelated marketing.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Information submitted through ELWOIC services may be securely stored in databases maintained by ELWOIC for operational purposes.

Stored information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • provide requested services;
  • maintain operational records;
  • respond to enquiries;
  • investigate misuse of services;
  • improve ELWOIC operations; or
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

Access to stored information is restricted to authorized personnel responsible for operating ELWOIC. ELWOIC takes reasonable steps to protect stored information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

7. Requesting Correction or Deletion of Information

Users may request correction or deletion of personal information previously submitted to ELWOIC. Before processing such requests, ELWOIC may take reasonable steps to verify the identity of the requesting individual.

Where legally and operationally possible, requested information will be corrected or permanently deleted within a reasonable period. Certain information may be retained where required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary for legitimate operational, security, or administrative purposes.

8. Cookies and Technical Information

ELWOIC does not currently use cookies for advertising, behavioural tracking, or personalized marketing.

However, infrastructure used to operate this website—including hosting providers, security services, content delivery networks (such as Cloudflare), domain providers, or similar technical services—may automatically collect limited technical information necessary for operating and securing the website.

Such information may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Date and time of access
  • Error logs
  • Security logs
  • Performance information

This information is generally used for:

  • Website security
  • Performance monitoring

  • Abuse prevention
  • Technical troubleshooting

Parents or legal guardians may contact ELWOIC regarding information submitted by a child and may request correction or deletion where appropriate.

Users should avoid submitting confidential or highly sensitive personal information unless specifically required by ELWOIC.

12. International Visitors

This website is operated from India. Visitors accessing ELWOIC from outside India acknowledge that information voluntarily submitted through this website may be processed and stored in India in accordance with applicable Indian laws.
