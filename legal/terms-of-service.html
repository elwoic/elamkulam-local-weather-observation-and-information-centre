2. About ELWOIC

Elamkulam Local Weather Observation & Information Centre (ELWOIC) is an independent, non-profit, student-operated community weather information initiative based in Elamkulam, Malappuram District, Kerala, India.

The primary objectives of ELWOIC include:

  • Promoting local weather awareness.
  • Encouraging responsible community participation in weather observation.
  • Providing educational weather information.
  • Supporting local weather monitoring.
  • Improving access to weather-related information for the public.

ELWOIC operates independently. While ELWOIC has been acknowledged by Elamkulam Grama Panchayat, and the Panchayat has expressed no objection to the operation of the initiative and the use of the name "Elamkulam Local Weather Observation & Information Centre," ELWOIC is not owned, operated, administered, or managed by:

  • Elamkulam Grama Panchayat
  • Government of Kerala
  • Government of India
  • India Meteorological Department (IMD)
  • Kerala State Disaster Management Authority (KSDMA)
  • Any other Government authority unless expressly stated.

Unless specifically announced through an official written agreement, no Government body assumes responsibility for the operation, services, forecasts, opinions, publications, or decisions of ELWOIC.

ELWOIC is not an official Government weather warning authority.

Information published by ELWOIC should not be interpreted as official emergency warnings, evacuation instructions, disaster management advice, or legally recognized public weather warnings. For official weather warnings, disaster information, cyclone bulletins, flood alerts, lightning warnings, or other emergency information, users should always refer to:
