Elamkulam Local Weather Observation And Information Centre
Online Since 14 Sept 2025
ELWOIC — Elamkulam Local Weather Observation And Information Centre — is an independent, student-run, non-profit initiative focused on local weather observation, monitoring, information, awareness and education in the Elamkulam area of Malappuram, Kerala. ELWOIC first came online on 14 September 2025 and operates independently on a non-commercial basis. ELWOIC is not a government organisation, government service or official government weather authority, and its information does not replace official warnings or advisories issued by competent authorities.

Official Status & Disclaimer

  • ELWOIC is an independent, student-run, non-profit initiative. It is not a government organisation, government department, statutory authority, local self-government institution, or government-owned service. It is not owned, operated, or controlled by the Government of Kerala, Government of India, Elamkulam Grama Panchayat, IMD, or Kerala Police.
  • The use of the geographical name “Elamkulam” identifies the area to which ELWOIC's activities relate and does not indicate government ownership or official government affiliation.
  • For official weather warnings and emergency advisories, always refer to the official IMD Kerala website or competent authorities.

ELWOIC weather alerts represent independent informational assessments and must not be interpreted as official government warnings unless explicitly stated.

Our Mission & Scope

Dedicated to local weather observation, monitoring, information sharing, reporting, awareness, and educational activities focused on Elamkulam and surrounding areas where general regional forecasts may not reflect micro-climatic conditions.

Operational Scope & Limitations

Data Sources, Accuracy & Guidelines
  • Data Sources: Combines observations from ELWOIC weather instruments, permitted APIs (AccuWeather, OpenWeatherMap, Open-Meteo, Windy), official IMD Kerala alerts, and verified local observations.
  • Accuracy & Limitations: No weather system can guarantee 100% accuracy in every circumstance. Weather conditions can change rapidly over short distances. Users should exercise caution and consult official authorities for high-consequence situations involving safety, property, or travel.
  • Independence: Support or equipment contributions from partners (Ecowitt, AirGradient, Beetel) do not imply ownership, endorsement, or formal institutional control over ELWOIC.
